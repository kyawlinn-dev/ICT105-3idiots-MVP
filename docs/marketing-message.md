## Responsible Marketing Statement

The Student Apartment Finder is a university prototype. Listing approval means that a record has passed the prototype administrator-review process; it does not guarantee current availability, property condition, landlord identity, or rental terms. Students should confirm important details directly before making a housing decision or payment.

Users should not submit passwords, identity documents, banking information, or unnecessary sensitive personal information through marketing links, feedback forms, roommate posts, or enquiries.
