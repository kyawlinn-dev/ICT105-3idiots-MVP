# Landing Page Content

## Project Title

Student Apartment Finder Platform

## Target User

The landing page is designed for university students, especially first-year students, international students, and students searching for off-campus accommodation near Rangsit University and Bangkok University.

## Prototype Value Review

### User Problem

Students often search through scattered Facebook groups, agents, chat messages, and personal recommendations to find accommodation. Listing information can be incomplete, outdated, or difficult to compare by price, distance, room type, facilities, and location.

### Current Prototype Value

The Student Apartment Finder brings approved apartment listings into one platform. Students can browse and filter listings, compare important details, view apartment photos and map locations, and contact landlords. Landlords can submit apartment records, while administrators can review and approve listings.

### Implemented Prototype Features

- Apartment search and filtering
- Apartment price, distance, room type, and facility information
- Apartment detail pages with photos and map locations
- Landlord listing submission
- Administrator listing review and approval
- Roommate posts and moderation
- Prototype dashboard and basic product metrics

### Communication Improvements Needed

The landing page should clearly explain:

- Who the platform is designed for
- How it reduces the time spent searching across scattered channels
- What an approved listing means
- Which prototype features are currently available
- How users can open and test the working prototype
- How submitted information will be used and protected

## Why the Target User Should Care

Students can compare approved apartment listings near their university in one place instead of searching through scattered and incomplete social media posts.

## Headline

Find student apartments near your university without searching everywhere.

## Subheadline

Compare approved apartment listings by rent, distance, room type, facilities, photos, and map location—all in one student-friendly platform.

## Problem Statement

Customer discovery in Lab 03 showed that students commonly depend on Facebook groups, rental agents, friends, seniors, and online communities when searching for accommodation. Because the information is spread across different channels, students can struggle to confirm prices, compare facilities, understand distance from campus, and determine whether a listing is still available.

## Solution Description

The Student Apartment Finder provides a centralized prototype where students can browse apartment listings near their university, apply search filters, compare important housing information, view detailed listings, and open confirmed map locations. The prototype also allows landlords to submit listings and administrators to review records before they are displayed publicly.

## Key Features

| Feature | Requirement ID | User Value | Prototype Screen / Module |
|---|---|---|---|
| Search and filter apartments | FR-06 | Students can narrow listings by relevant housing criteria | Apartments page |
| View apartment details | FR-07 | Students can compare price, distance, room information, photos, facilities, and location | Apartment detail page |
| Submit apartment listings | FR-03 | Landlords can add structured accommodation information | Landlord Add Listing form |
| Review and approve listings | FR-08/FR-09 | Administrators can manage the quality and status of submitted listings | Admin Listings screen |
| Create and review roommate posts | FR-13 | Students can look for compatible roommates through moderated posts | Roommates module |

## Benefits

- Reduces time spent searching across unrelated channels
- Makes apartment information easier to compare
- Gives students access to photos and map locations before visiting
- Provides a structured process for landlord submissions
- Uses administrator approval to improve listing quality
- Supports students who have limited knowledge of the surrounding area

## Responsible Data Message

The Student Apartment Finder only requests information needed to provide apartment listings, roommate posts, and enquiries. Users should avoid submitting passwords, identity documents, financial information, or unnecessary sensitive personal details. Public information should be reviewed before publication, and private contact information should only be used for the intended housing enquiry or account function.
