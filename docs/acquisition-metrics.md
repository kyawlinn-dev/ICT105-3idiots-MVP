# Acquisition Metrics

## Purpose

These metrics will measure early user awareness, landing-page engagement, prototype usage, feedback participation, and conversion for the Student Apartment Finder. Results will be recorded by outreach channel so the team can identify which channel and message attract the most relevant student users.

## Measurement Period

The initial measurement period will begin after the Lab 12 landing page and CTA are published. The team will record results by date and channel in `data/acquisition-metrics-template.csv`.

## Required Metrics

| Metric ID | Metric Name | Metric Type | What It Measures | Formula / Counting Method | Data Source | Initial Success Target |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| AM-01 | Landing-page views | Awareness | Number of times users open the landing page | Count all recorded landing-page visits | Landing-page counter, localStorage, or manual tracking | At least 30 views |
| AM-02 | CTA clicks | Action | Number of times users click the main Browse Apartments button | Count main CTA click events | Landing-page script or manual test log | At least 10 clicks |
| AM-03 | CTA click-through rate | Action | Percentage of landing-page visitors who click the main CTA | CTA clicks / landing-page views × 100 | Acquisition CSV | At least 30% |
| AM-04 | Demo attempts | Action | Number of users who open and try the apartment prototype | Count prototype visits originating from the landing page | Prototype link log or manual tracking | At least 8 attempts |
| AM-05 | Feedback responses | Feedback | Number of users who submit useful prototype feedback | Count completed feedback responses | Feedback form or spreadsheet | At least 5 responses |
| AM-06 | Interested testers | Conversion | Number of users who agree to test again or join further validation | Count confirmed testers or sign-ups | Feedback form or tester list | At least 5 testers |
| AM-07 | Interest conversion rate | Conversion | Percentage of landing-page visitors who become interested testers | Interested testers / landing-page views × 100 | Acquisition CSV | At least 15% |
| AM-08 | Channel contribution | Awareness and conversion | Number of views, clicks, demo attempts, feedback responses, and testers generated by each outreach channel | Compare totals grouped by channel | Acquisition CSV | Identify at least one effective channel |

## Metric Categories

### Awareness Metric

`AM-01 Landing-page views` measures whether the selected channels successfully expose the Student Apartment Finder to early users.

### Action Metrics

`AM-02 CTA clicks`, `AM-03 CTA click-through rate`, and `AM-04 Demo attempts` measure whether visitors understand the landing-page message and take the next step.

### Feedback Metric

`AM-05 Feedback responses` measures whether users are willing to provide information that can improve the prototype and landing page.

### Conversion Metrics

`AM-06 Interested testers` and `AM-07 Interest conversion rate` measure whether visitors become meaningful early users rather than only opening the page.

## Formulas

### CTA Click-Through Rate

```text
CTA click-through rate = CTA clicks / Landing-page views × 100
```

### Demo Attempt Rate

```text
Demo attempt rate = Demo attempts / Landing-page views × 100
```

### Feedback Response Rate

```text
Feedback response rate = Feedback responses / Demo attempts × 100
```

### Interest Conversion Rate

```text
Interest conversion rate = Interested testers / Landing-page views × 100
```

If the denominator is zero, the rate will be recorded as `Not Available` instead of dividing by zero.

## Data Collection Method

The team will record one row for each date and outreach channel. The tracking data will include:

- Date
- Outreach channel
- Landing-page views
- CTA clicks
- Demo attempts
- Feedback responses
- Interested testers
- Notes about the campaign or technical problems

The landing-page script may use localStorage or a simulated counter for prototype demonstration. Actual outreach results may also be recorded manually in the CSV after checking the relevant channel or feedback form.

## Responsible Data Collection

The acquisition tracker will record counts rather than names whenever possible. If users voluntarily join further testing, contact information should be stored separately with limited access and should not be committed to the public GitHub repository. The public CSV must not contain passwords, student identification numbers, private email addresses, phone numbers, identity documents, financial information, or precise personal locations.

## Interpretation and Decisions

- If landing-page views are low, the team will use a more relevant student channel or improve the visibility of the shared link.
- If views are high but CTA clicks are low, the team will revise the headline, benefits, or CTA placement.
- If CTA clicks are high but demo attempts are low, the team will check the link and simplify the transition to the prototype.
- If demo attempts are high but feedback is low, the team will shorten the feedback process and explain why feedback is valuable.
- If one channel performs better than the others, the team will prioritize that channel for the next validation round.
- If users misunderstand approved listings as guaranteed accommodation, the team will make the prototype limitation statement more visible.
