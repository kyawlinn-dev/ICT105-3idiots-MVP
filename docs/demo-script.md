# Demo Script

## Demo Objective

The purpose of this demonstration is to prove that the Student Apartment Finder Platform provides a working pathway for students to find and compare apartments near their university.

The demonstration will show how students can browse and filter approved listings, view apartment details, photographs, prices, facilities, availability, and map locations, and contact a landlord. It will also show how landlords submit listings and how an administrator reviews platform records and metrics.
