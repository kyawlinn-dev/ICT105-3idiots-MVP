# Sales Scenario

## 1. Project Title

Student Apartment Finder Platform

## 2. Target User / Customer

- **Primary users:** First-year students, international students, and other university students searching for accommodation near campus.
- **Secondary users:** Apartment landlords who want to advertise available rooms to students.
- **Decision-maker or admin role:** Platform administrators who review apartment listings and roommate posts before making them available to users.

## 3. Situation / Context

A first-year or international student needs to find an apartment near Rangsit University or Bangkok University before the semester begins. The student searches through Facebook groups, rental websites, agents, and recommendations from friends. Because the information is spread across different sources, comparing apartments takes a long time.

The student wants to compare monthly rent, distance from the university, room type, facilities, photographs, availability, and map location before contacting a landlord.

## 4. Pain Point

Apartment information is often scattered, incomplete, outdated, or difficult to compare. Students may not know the real distance from campus, whether a room is still available, which facilities are included, or whether the landlord information is reliable.

This creates confusion, wastes time, and makes it difficult for students—especially first-year and international students—to make a confident housing decision.
## 5. Product Introduction

The Student Apartment Finder Platform introduces students to one centralized place where they can browse apartment listings near their university. Students can search and filter listings, compare prices and facilities, view photographs and map locations, check availability, and review detailed apartment information.

Landlords can submit apartment listings, while the platform administrator can review and approve listings before they become visible to students. The platform also provides a roommate-finding feature for students who want to share accommodation.

## 6. Value Message

For university students who experience difficulty finding reliable and comparable accommodation information, our Student Apartment Finder Platform helps them find suitable apartments near campus by providing centralized listings with prices, distance, facilities, photographs, availability, landlord contact details, and map locations.
